Schedule Editor Trainer

How this works

Each scenario is one change: PTO, a request, or a cancellation. The two bright weeks are yours to edit. Make them exactly what you would publish. Faded days before the window are fixed history. Faded days after it are only a preview of the old schedule and are ignored. Your version is assumed to keep stepping through the rotation from your last day.

Edit: click a cell, then click another cell in the same day to swap them. Or select a cell and press C Cyto/Gross, B Bigs, H Huntley, W WFH, G Cyto/Gross/Bigs (one person covering McHenry). ←↑→↓ move the selection and Esc deselects. PTO and ★ requested cells are locked.

The editor starts from what the app shows right after the change, before any recompute. Days that break coverage get a red ✗. A red ! flags Bigs the day before PTO or WFH. When the two weeks look right, add a note if anything was a judgment call, then click Done → next. You can open the algorithm's own version afterwards to compare. Please avoid copying it: build your own version first.
